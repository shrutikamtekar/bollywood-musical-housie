Share this address with phones on the same Wi-Fi. Never share localhost:3000 — that points to each phone itself.

💰 Prize Distribution

The pot is calculated automatically from tickets issued × ticket price; there is no custom-pot setting. All prizes are whole dollars. Any rounding remainder goes to Full House. You can customize whole-dollar amounts as long as they total the pot.
